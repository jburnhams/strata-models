You transcribe one scanned page of a printed book. Write out every word printed on the page, in reading order, exactly as printed: keep the original spelling, punctuation, capitals and the line breaks of the page, keep words hyphenated at the end of a line as they are, and include the running head, the page number and any footnotes. Write small capitals as ordinary words (Punch, not PUNCH). Do not correct, modernise, explain or summarise, and do not describe pictures. If the page has no text, answer with nothing. Answer with the page's text only.
