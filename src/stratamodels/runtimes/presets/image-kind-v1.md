Say what kind of picture this is. It was cut from a scanned book (often an old one), so it may be printed on yellowed paper, show
the page's grain or the print from the other side, or include a little of the page around it.

The kind is how the picture was MADE, not how this book printed it: an 18th-century engraving reproduced in a modern book is still an
engraving, a photograph of a puppet is a photograph. Look closely at the marks: parallel and crossed lines (engraving, etching), white lines
cut out of black (wood engraving), bold coarse black shapes (woodcut), a regular grid of dots (a halftone of a photograph), soft grainy tone
(lithograph), washes of colour over black lines (hand-coloured), solid printed colour (colour print). When most of the picture is a page of
text with only a sliver of illustration, the kind is text.

Kinds (choose exactly one):
- line-drawing: a pen-and-ink drawing or outline: clean lines, little or no shading
- engraving: a copper or steel engraving or an etching: fine lines, shading by parallel and cross-hatched lines
- wood-engraving: a wood engraving: white lines cut from black, fine tints, as in Victorian books and magazines
- woodcut: a woodcut or chapbook cut: bold, coarse black lines and areas, simple shapes
- hand-coloured: a black-line print (engraving, etching, lithograph) coloured by hand with washes
- lithograph: a lithograph or crayon drawing: soft grainy tones as well as lines
- colour-print: printed in colours: a chromolithograph, colour woodblock or modern colour illustration
- tonal-drawing: a pencil, charcoal, wash or watercolour drawing with continuous tones
- painting: a reproduction of a painting (oil, watercolour, gouache)
- diagram: a diagram, map, plan, chart or technical drawing
- ornament: a printer's ornament: headpiece, tailpiece, border, rule, decorated initial, fleuron
- silhouette: a silhouette or shadow figure: flat black shapes
- photograph: a photograph with continuous tones (black and white, sepia or colour)
- halftone: a photograph printed through a halftone screen: a pattern of dots visible up close
- pattern: a repeating or decorative surface: marbled paper, endpaper, textile, wallpaper
- binding: part of the book itself: cloth or leather cover, spine, page edges
- handwriting: handwriting: an inscription, signature, note or manuscript
- stamp: a stamp, bookplate, library label, seal or barcode
- text: printed text only (a page or block of type, a caption)
- blank: blank or nearly blank paper

colour: true when the picture itself has colour (printed or painted), false for black or a single ink on tinted paper.

What the app knows about it: {context}

Answer as JSON: {"kind": one of the kinds, "colour": true or false, "why": one short sentence naming what you saw}.
